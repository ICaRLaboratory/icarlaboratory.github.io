Research

From an inequality on paper
to a manipulator that holds its line.

We develop stability analysis and controller synthesis for time-delay, sampled-data, networked and descriptor systems, and we hold that work to a practical bar: the condition has to be solvable and the controller has to run on the target board. Robotics and embedded systems are where it gets tested and where the next problem usually comes from.
