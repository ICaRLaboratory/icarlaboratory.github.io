Spectral radius —
Settling time —

The radius is the loop linearised about the target pose: under one and it settles, over one and it does not. Every run plays the full twenty seconds, and the mark on the record is where both joints first held a 2% band — early with a flat line after it when the pair is well tuned, nowhere at all when it is not.
